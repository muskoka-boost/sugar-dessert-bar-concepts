The story

A home bakery in Orillia, now in its sixth year.

Sugar Dessert Bar opened in 2020 as a home-based bakery. Co-owner Mark Robillard specialises in custom cake design — birthday and wedding cakes — along with cupcakes, decorated cookies and decadent desserts.

“We always put our heart and soul into cake making and I always want the customer to have a jaw-dropping experience.”

Mark Robillard, to OrilliaMatters, January 2022
  1. 2021

    Food Network Champions

    Mark, Mandy Collinson and Eleanor Roxas won The Big Bake: Holiday with a rocket-ship cake.

  2. 2026

    Best Bakery in Orillia

    Voted by the community in the Canadian Choice Awards. “It’s really cool because it’s your community that’s voting for you. So that definitely means a lot.” — Mark, January 2026

  3. 2026

    Most Valuable Partner

    Delicious Days of Summer, Downtown Orillia.

How ordering works

Three quiet steps to your cake.

Now booking into November and December. Ordering is by direct message or email.

  1. I

    Share your idea

    Send a message on Instagram or Facebook, or email. Tell us the date, the occasion, how many guests, and any theme, colours or photos you love.

  2. II

    Mark designs it with you

    Together you settle flavours, fillings, colours and style, until the design feels exactly like your occasion.

  3. III

    Pickup in Orillia

    Your cake is ready to collect. Orillia, Ontario — pickup details arranged when you order.
