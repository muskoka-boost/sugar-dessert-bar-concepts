## Rules
- Single folder per version: index.html + style.css + main.js (or inline). No build step, no frameworks. Google Fonts OK.
- Include `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and a small unobtrusive "Concept design by Muskoka Digital Boost — not the bakery's official site" line in the footer only (no big banners).
- Every version must have: hero, work gallery (real photos, with a lightbox), what they make (custom cakes / cupcakes & cookies / dessert tables & seasonal — NO prices), story + awards (Food Network, Best Bakery, MVP), reviews (real quotes above, never invented), upcoming markets, how ordering works (1 share your idea → 2 Mark designs it with you → 3 pickup; keep claims generic, no turnaround promises), enquiry form that builds a mailto: to sugarbarorillia@gmail.com (fields: name, phone/email, date, occasion, servings, idea) with a copy-to-clipboard fallback; never claim it was "sent". Call/email/Instagram/Facebook links.
- Mobile-first, responsive at 390 / 768 / 1440, no horizontal overflow, 44px tap targets, focus states, prefers-reduced-motion, alt text, lazy-loading.
- Avoid AI slop: no emoji icons, no generic 3-icon feature grids, no purple tech gradients, no fake stats.
