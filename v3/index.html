The Champion's Story

Out of this world.

Sugar Dessert Bar opened in Orillia in 2020 as a home-based custom cake bakery. A year later, it was on Food Network.

On The Big Bake: Holiday, shot in 2021, Mark Robillard, Mandy Collinson and Eleanor Roxas took on the competition with a rocket-ship cake — and won. Food Network Champions, from a home kitchen in Orillia.

In 2026 the community voted Sugar Best Bakery in Orillia in the Canadian Choice Awards, and Downtown Orillia named it Most Valuable Partner for Delicious Days of Summer.

“We always put our heart and soul into cake making and I always want the customer to have a jaw-dropping experience.”

Mark Robillard, OrilliaMatters, Jan 2022
