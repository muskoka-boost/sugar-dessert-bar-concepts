Meet the baker

Meet Mark,
maker of jaw-drops.

Mark Robillard co-owns Sugar Dessert Bar, a home-based custom cake and dessert bakery that opened in Orillia in 2020. Six years in, it’s still birthday cakes, wedding cakes, cupcakes, decorated cookies and decadent desserts, each one designed with you.

“We always put our heart and soul into cake making and I always want the customer to have a jaw-dropping experience.”

Mark Robillard, to OrilliaMatters (Jan 2022)

How to order

Now booking into November and December. Ordering is by DM or email.

  1. 1

    Share your idea

    Send the date, the occasion, roughly how many people and the vibe. A theme, a photo or a few loose ideas all work.

  2. 2

    Mark designs it with you

    You chat through flavours, fillings, colours and styles until it feels exactly right.

  3. 3

    Pick up & party

    Collect your cake in Orillia (pickup details are arranged when you order), then go steal the party.
